  # Cyber Security
  
  Protect your business against exploitable network vulnerabilities with industry‑standard procedures and technology.
  
  ## Application Security
  As attacks have become frequent and focused on the application layer, threatening customers' critical data and operations, SLTMobitel introduces Application Security to safeguard your enterprise. Attackers target applications and APIs to exploit vulnerabilities, abuse business logic, compromise customer accounts, and commit large‑scale fraud. SLTMobitel Application Security deploys an ironclad application‑layer security in your systems, safeguarding your business revenue and customer growth.
  
  ## Network Security
  Protect your business against exploitable network vulnerabilities with industry‑validated IPS technology offered by SLTMobitel Network Security. The service provides application‑layer visibility and protection, unified threat management features, and other measures to mitigate threats such as data leaks, virus attacks, and intrusions.
  
  ## Cloud Security
  SLTMobitel introduces the Cloud Security Service, a solution built on industry‑standard procedures and technology that secures your enterprise's cloud computing environments against external and insider cyber threats. It consistently protects infrastructure hosting, data hosting, and cloud products from cyber attacks.
  
  ## DDoS Protection Service
  SLTMobitel offers DDoS Protection Services against distributed denial‑of‑service attacks to prevent business disruptions, monetary losses, data loss, productivity loss, and reputation damage. The service provides resistance to large‑volume and varied DDoS attacks, ensuring continuity for your enterprise.
