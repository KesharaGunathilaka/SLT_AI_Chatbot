  # Milestones
  
  **2020**
  - SLT partners with Fon Wireless Limited to create Community Wi‑Fi networks.
  
  **2019**
  - SLT steps into minimizing carbon footprint under the “Earth is Calling, Are You Listening” initiative.
  
  **2018**
  - Opening of the state‑of‑the‑art tier 3 “National Data Center” in Sri Lanka.
  
  **2017**
  - Successfully field‑tested pre‑5G LTE advanced pro technology in South Asia.
  - Opening of National Telecommunication Museum, Padukka.
  
  **2016**
  - Introduced SEA‑ME‑WE 5 Submarine Cable System.
  
  **2015**
  - Launch of Fibre Optic Broadband (FTTx) service.
  - Launch of National Cloud platform (AKAZA).
  - Increased coverage of SLT Carrier Grade Wi‑Fi network to >200 sites island‑wide.
  - Terabyte Broadband Data packages introduced.
  - Phase 5 of NGN migration completed.
  
  **2014**
  - SLT inked USD 415 million ICT investment agreement with the BOI.
  - SLT incorporated “SLT Property Management (Pvt) Ltd.”
  - Launched “Sisu‑Connect” service to strengthen child‑parent communication.
  - Launched fixed 4G/LTE technology.
  - Launched breakthrough cloud computing service “Akaza”.
  - Partnered with 15 global telcos for SEA‑ME‑WE 5 submarine cable.
  - Launched revolutionary 100 Mbps FTTH ultra‑fast broadband.
  - Launched world‑class carrier‑grade public Wi‑Fi hotspots.
  - Connected “Delft Island” in Jaffna to the information super‑highway.
  - Upgraded the national research and education network (LEARN).
  
  **2013**
  - 100 Mbps super‑fast broadband internet went live in Sri Lanka.
  - Announced soft‑launch of VDSL2 technology.
  
  **2012**
  - Broadband speed enhanced up to 4× (up to 16 Mbps).
  - Launched TV Banking service on PeoTV platform.
  - Opened newest Teleshop at heritage CTO building in Colombo Fort.
  - Launched Technology Experience Centre.
  - Deployed FTTx technology with i‑Sri Lanka project.
  
  **2011**
  - Sky Network Private Limited launched Skymax, the first WiMAX 16e broadband service in Sri Lanka.
  - Recorded Rs. 50 bn revenue.
  - Launched i‑Sri Lanka network modernisation project.
  
  **2010**
  - Launched new corporate vision, mission & values.
  - Expanded fibre‑optic information superhighway to the Northern Peninsula.
  - Received ISO 9001:2008 certification.
  - Reached 200,000th broadband customer.
  
  **2009**
  - Launched Prestige Card, a loyalty reward card for personal solution customers.
  - Successfully deployed IPv6 in ISP network.
  
  **2008**
  - Boosted distance education through broadband connectivity to the National Online Distance Education System.
  - Launched Megaline and upgraded SLTnet International bandwidth to 3 Gbps.
  
  **2007**
  - Mobitel launched M3.
  - SLT launched PeoTV, Sri Lanka’s first IPTV service.
  - Obtained BOI status & signed BOI agreement for Stage III GSM and 3G expansion.
  - Launched volume‑based broadband internet package.
  - Hosted SANOG IX, South Asia’s largest networking conference.
  
  **2006**
  - SLT Hong Kong launched.
  - Inaugurated Dhiraagu‑SLT submarine cable system (Maldives‑Sri Lanka) and Bharat Lanka Submarine Cable System with BSNL.
  - Achieved highest international information security standards.
  - Inaugurated local operations of SEA‑ME‑WE 4 submarine cable system.
  
  **2005**
  - Launched SLT Citylink CDMA network in Anuradhapura.
  - Focused on intelligent business solutions.
  - Introduced e‑procurement system (eTENDER).
  
  **2004**
  - Received Fitch SL “AAA” rating.
  - Launched state‑of‑the‑art Internet Data Centre.
  - Signed MOU with BSNL of India for submarine cable system.
  
  **2003**
  - Shares first traded on Colombo Stock Exchange.
  - Broadband connections shook up the marketplace.
  - Market liberalisation of international services.
  
  **2002**
  - Acquired remaining 60 % share of Mobitel (Pvt) Ltd., a mobile communications company.
  
  **2001**
  - Completed Rs. 7,800 million Regional Telecommunication Development Project.
  - Completed Rs. 44 billion infrastructure development project.
  
  **2000**
  - Issued Rs. 1.5 billion debenture.
  - First optical fibre ring connected 15 cities.
  
  **1999**
  - Customer base reached 600,000.
  - Linked up with SEA‑ME‑WE III digital fibre‑optic submarine cable.
  
  **1998**
  - Customer base reached 500,000.
  - Centralised fault reporting to universal number 121.
  - Opened Teleshop – Customer Care Centres.
  
  **1997**
  - Sri Lanka Telecom privatised with collaboration of Nippon Telegraph & Telephone Corporation (NTT) of Japan.
  
  **1996**
  - Introduced Internet and E‑mail services.
  - Converted into a public company.
  
  **1991**
  - Sri Lanka Telecom Corporation formed.
  
  **1980**
  - Postal and Telegraph services separated, forming Telecommunications and Postal departments.
  
  **1976**
  - First Earth Satellite Station (Standard A) commissioned in Padukka.
  
  **1963**
  - Telex service introduced.
  
  **1951**
  - Post and Telecommunications Department of Ceylon took over operations of Cable & Wireless Company.
  
  **1941**
  - International operations handed over to Cable & Wireless Company.
  
  **1935**
  - International Telephone Service inaugurated.
  
  **1911**
  - Central Telegraph Office opened.
  
  **1896**
  - Government of Ceylon took over Oriental Telephone Company with manually operated exchange.
  
  **1880**
  - Postal and Telegraph services amalgamated under Government of Ceylon.
  
  **1858**
  - First telegraphic circuit established between Galle and Colombo; first international telegraph communication between Ceylon and India.
