  * #####  Airline Tickets 
This is a joint service offered to Megaline and Citylik customers in collaboration with Mobitel. Now you can simply reserve airline tickets from your SLT telephone simply by dialing 1265.
    * Services offered
    * Reservation of air tickets of Sri Lankan Airline and Mihin Lanka.
    * Reservations of Holiday packages organized by Sri Lankan Airline and Mihin Lanka.
    * How to reserve tickets
    * Call **1265** from your SLT telephone and contact our agent.
    * How to purchase the ticket
    * You have to visit nearest Mobitel Sales Outlet to purchase the ticket.
    * Charges
    * **1265** calling charges : Rs.8.00 per minute.
    * Standard air ticket charge.
    * Terms and conditions
    * The customer is given a grace period to purchase the ticket. If it is not purchased within that period, then the reservation will be canceled.
    * Customer can terminate the agreement at any time by calling customer support hotline on 1212 and making a request.
    * The customer shall provide a mobile or fixed telephone number to send the confirmation SMS.
    * The pricing of reservation details are not included in to the SLT bill.
    * Above charges are exclusive of taxes. Relevant taxes will be applicable at the time of purchase.
