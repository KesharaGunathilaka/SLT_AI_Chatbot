  # Vision, Mission & Values
  
  ## Our Vision
  "All Sri Lankans seamlessly connected with world-class information, communication and entertainment services."
  
  ## Our Mission
  "Your trusted and proven partner for innovative and exciting communication experiences delivered with passion, quality and commitment"
  
  ## Our Values
  1. **Customer Caring** – We put our customers at the centre of everything we do
  2. **Trustworthy** – We are true to our promises
  3. **Innovative** – We continuously invent new opportunities through creative thinking
  4. **Responsive** – We are ready to listen and act promptly
  5. **Teamwork** – We are one team with a common purpose to achieve common goals
  6. **Excellence** – We are committed to exceptional performance
  7. **Results Driven** – We are committed to enhancing shareholder value
  
  ## Awards & Recognitions
  - 2009‑2010: 7th in the Business Today 'Top Twenty' Awards
  - Top Ten Best Corporate Citizens (CSR & Sustainable Business)
  - SAFA – Best presented accounts in Communication and IT sector
  - 2013: ISO/IEC 27001:2005 certification
  - 2013: Gold award in the ICASL Annual Report Awards
  - 2013: Bronze Award – Innovative Brand of the Year at SLIM Brand Excellence Awards
  - 2013: ISO 9001:2008 certification
  - 2013: Top 10 of the LMD 100
  - 2012: Gold Winner of 49th Annual Report Awards of CA
  - 2012: HRM Awards – Silver Award
  - 2011: SLIM / Nielsen Power of the People Award – Fixed line/CDMA Telecom Services Provider of the Year
  - 2010: Gold Award at Vision Awards (Annual Report)
  - 2010: Most Respected Entities in Sri Lanka – Hall of Fame
  - 2010: National Business Excellence Awards – Runner‑up: Infrastructure & Utilities Sector
  - 2010: Gold award at the Chartered Accounts Annual Report Awards (Telecommunication Sector)
  - 2009/10: Second on the 'LEADERBOARD' for the financial year 2009/10
