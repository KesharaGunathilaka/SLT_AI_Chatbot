###  [](javascript:void\(0\))Hosting Services - hostingcub.com
SLT-MOBITEL's shared hosting services have been migrated into a dedicated platform and are now available to the public on [**www.hostingcub.com**](https://www.hostingcub.com/). The platform offers Shared Web Hosting, Virtual Private Server (VPS) and Domain Name Registration services. The platform is hosted at SLT-MOBITEL's state-of-the-art data centres with 24x7 monitoring and high availability.
