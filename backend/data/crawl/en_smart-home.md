### SLT-MOBITEL Smart Home Service
  * SLT-MOBITEL Smart Home service enables you to use internet-connected devices for remote monitoring and management of your electronic appliances, such as remote surveillance, heat ventilation, air-conditioning and even your lighting. An array of IoT devices converts your home into a smart home which can be controlled easily from anywhere.
